title: "Escalations"
description: "How tickets are escalated to humans and where to review them"
---

Escalations are raised automatically when the AI or the SLA monitor decides a ticket needs a human, or when a user presses **Talk to Staff**. They are collected in a queue at **Dashboard → Escalations**.

## What Triggers an Escalation

| Trigger | When |
|---------|------|
| **Low confidence** | The Knowledge Base could not answer confidently (see auto-escalation below) |
| **Explicit request** | The user asked for a human, or pressed **Talk to Staff** |
| **SLA breach** | An SLA target was missed |
| **Sentiment** | Negative tone such as anger or frustration was detected |
| **Priority** | A high or critical priority ticket |
| **No response** | Staff have not responded in time |
| **Sensitive topic** | Billing, account access, security, or legal topics |

## Settings That Control It

These live in the server configuration:

- **Knowledge Base → Auto-escalate on low confidence**: raise an escalation when the AI cannot find a good answer.
- **Knowledge Base → Ping staff on escalation**: mention the staff role in the ticket.
- **Knowledge Base → Escalation duration**: how many days the AI stays out of an escalated ticket (0 to 30, default 7; 0 means permanently).
- **SLA → Escalation rules**: the Warning and Critical rules with ping, DM, channel alert, and reassign actions. See [SLA Management](/configuration/sla-management).

<Note>
  Sentiment-based escalation needs the **Enterprise** plan; the other AI triggers need **Pro** or higher.
</Note>

## The Escalation Queue

**Dashboard → Escalations** lists every escalation with its trigger reason and status, and can be filtered by status and reason or searched.

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | Nobody has picked it up |
| **Assigned** | A staff member is assigned |
| **In progress** | Being handled |
| **Resolved** | Closed out |

Queue statistics and analytics are available for the last 24 hours, 7 days, or 30 days.
