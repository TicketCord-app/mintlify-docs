---
title: "SLA Management"
description: "Define and track Service Level Agreements for your support"
---

SLA Management sets first-response and resolution targets per priority, warns staff before targets are missed, and records whether each ticket met its SLA.

<Info>
  SLA Management is an **Enterprise** feature. [Upgrade your plan](https://ticketcord.com/pricing) to access it.
</Info>

## Setting Up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the SLA tab">
    Go to **Dashboard → Bots**, pick your bot, choose the server, and open the **SLA** tab. Turn **SLA Management** on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set targets per priority">
    Each priority has a **first response** target (1 to 1,440 minutes) and a **resolution** target (1 to 10,080 minutes).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose notifications">
    Pick a notification channel, and whether to notify the staff role and the first responder. Optionally move the ticket to the top of its category on warning, and notify when a target is met.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Default Targets

| Priority | First response | Resolution |
|----------|----------------|------------|
| Low | 240 min | 2,880 min (48 h) |
| Medium | 120 min | 1,440 min (24 h) |
| High | 60 min | 480 min (8 h) |
| Critical | 15 min | 120 min (2 h) |

<Note>
  SLA priorities are Low, Medium, High, and Critical. A ticket set to **Urgent** with `/priority` uses the Critical targets.
</Note>

## Escalation Rules

Two built-in rules fire as a target approaches:

| Rule | Default trigger | Adjustable range |
|------|-----------------|------------------|
| **Warning** | 75% of the target elapsed | 50% to 99% |
| **Critical** | 90% of the target elapsed | 50% to 99% (always above Warning) |

For each rule choose who to notify (roles and users), an optional delay in minutes, and one or more actions:

| Action | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Ping** | Mentions the chosen roles and users in the ticket |
| **DM** | Sends them a direct message |
| **Channel alert** | Posts to the notification channel |
| **Reassign** | Reassigns the ticket |

**Auto-escalation** can repeat the escalation up to a maximum level (1 to 10) with a delay between levels (1 to 1,440 minutes).

**Breach notifications** are sent when a target is missed; you can include ticket details, customer info, and performance context.

## Business Hours

Enable **Business hours only** to pause SLA timers outside your configured [Business Hours](/configuration/business-hours). This is the only way timers pause; there is no manual pause.

## Tracking and Reports

- Each ticket records an SLA status: **met**, **breached**, **partial breach**, or **active**.
- **Dashboard → Analytics** shows SLA breach warnings and staff SLA misses.
- **Dashboard → Escalations** lists escalations raised for SLA breaches.
- SLA analytics can be filtered by date range, priority, and status.
