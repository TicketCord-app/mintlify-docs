---
title: "Business Hours"
description: "Configure operating hours for your support team"
---

Business Hours tell TicketCord when your team is available. Outside those hours the bot can block new tickets or let them through with an off-hours notice, and SLA timers can pause.

<Info>
  Business Hours is an **Enterprise** feature. [Upgrade your plan](https://ticketcord.com/pricing) to access it.
</Info>

## Setting Up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the server configuration">
    Go to **Dashboard → Bots**, pick your bot, choose the server, and open the **Business Hours** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a timezone">
    Pick your team's timezone, or enable **Auto-detect timezone**. Daylight saving time is handled automatically because schedules use IANA timezones.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the weekly schedule">
    For each day, enable it and set one start time and one end time (24-hour `HH:MM`). Disabled days are treated as closed. Overnight ranges such as `22:00` to `06:00` are supported.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add holidays (optional)">
    Add dates (`YYYY-MM-DD`) with a name. Holidays are full-day closures; mark one as **recurring** to close on that date every year.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the off-hours notice">
    Set a title (up to 100 characters) and description (up to 500 characters), or design a Components V2 container. Choose whether to **show the next opening time** and whether to **allow ticket creation** outside hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Happens Outside Hours

| Setting | Behaviour |
|---------|-----------|
| **Allow ticket creation** on | The ticket is created and the off-hours notice is posted in it |
| **Allow ticket creation** off | The ticket is not created; the user sees the notice with error code TC018 |

The notice supports a `{nextOpenTime}` placeholder that renders as a Discord timestamp when **show next opening time** is enabled.

## On-Call Fallback

When nobody is on call (see `/on-call`), choose what happens with new tickets:

| Mode | Behaviour |
|------|-----------|
| **Block** | New tickets are refused |
| **Add all staff** (default) | All staff roles are added to the ticket |
| **Add all with warning** | All staff roles are added and a warning is posted |

## SLA and Business Hours

In the **SLA** tab, enable **Business hours only** to pause SLA timers outside your schedule. Otherwise SLA timers run around the clock. There is no per-priority calendar.

<Warning>
  Auto-close timers count wall-clock time and are not affected by business hours.
</Warning>

## Limits

- One schedule and one timezone per server
- One time range per day
- Holidays are always full-day closures

## Troubleshooting

### Tickets still open outside hours

Check that Business Hours is enabled and that **Allow ticket creation** is off. Verify the timezone matches your team's location.

### SLA timers keep running at night

Enable **Business hours only** in the SLA settings.
