The dashboard at [ticketcord.com/dashboard](https://ticketcord.com/dashboard) is where you add bots, configure servers, and work with tickets. Sign in with Discord.

## Layout

- **Sidebar**: the main navigation, grouped into General, Intelligence, Operations, and Account. Your account menu sits at the bottom of the sidebar.
- **Top bar**: breadcrumbs for the page you are on and the language switcher.

## Sidebar Sections

| Section | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Dashboard** | Total tickets, new today, active tickets, average response time, a ticket graph, and recent tickets |
| **Bots** | Your custom bots: status, servers, credentials, restart, delete |
| **Servers** | Every server your bots are in; opens that server's configuration |
| **Tickets** | Search, filter, and manage tickets across your bots |
| **Analytics** | Support performance for the last 7, 30, or 90 days |
| **AI Usage** | Token usage for AI features |
| **MCP** | Connect AI assistants such as Claude Code to your TicketCord data |
| **Approvals** | Pending and decided approval requests |
| **Escalations** | Tickets escalated to humans |
| **Billing** | Plan, payment history, and the Stripe billing portal |
| **Support** | Contact options and your data rights |

## Server Configuration

Everything about how a bot behaves in one server (staff roles, panels and embeds, welcome messages, closing, auto-close, transcripts, quick replies, AI and Knowledge Base, business hours, SLA, approvals, scheduled reports, webhooks, language) is configured per server: **Bots → your bot → Servers → the server**.

## Account Menu

At the bottom of the sidebar: **Billing**, **Replay onboarding**, **Appearance** (light, dark, or system), and **Sign out**. Your name and avatar come from Discord.

See [Keyboard Shortcuts](/features/keyboard-shortcuts) for the palette and navigation keys.
