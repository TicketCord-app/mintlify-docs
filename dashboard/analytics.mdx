---
title: "Analytics & Reports"
description: "Understand your support performance with analytics and reports"
---

**Dashboard → Analytics** shows how your support is performing.

<Info>
  Analytics is available on **Basic** and higher.
</Info>

## Time Range

Choose the last **7**, **30**, or **90 days**.

## What You See

| Section | Contents |
|---------|----------|
| **Overview** | Total tickets, open tickets, closed tickets, tickets today and yesterday, average first response time, average resolution time |
| **Staff performance** | Tickets handled, tickets closed, messages, average response and resolution time, ratings, SLA misses, recent tickets |
| **Top resolvers** | Staff who closed the most tickets |
| **Category breakdown** | Tickets per category |
| **Busiest hours** | When tickets are opened |
| **Repeat users** | Users who open many tickets |
| **Daily stats** | Tickets created per day |
| **SLA** | Breach warnings and staff SLA misses (Enterprise) |
| **Sentiment** | Customer mood over time (Enterprise) |

AI token usage is on its own page, **Dashboard → AI Usage**.

## Scheduled Reports

Enterprise servers can email these metrics on a schedule. See [Scheduled Reports](/features/scheduled-reports).

<Note>
  There is no custom report builder, widget layout, or analytics export. Ticket exports are available from the Tickets page.
</Note>
