TicketCord has no separate settings page. Account options live in the sidebar account menu and the top bar.

## Appearance

Open the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **System**.

## Language

Use the language switcher in the top bar. The dashboard is available in 12 languages: English, Čeština, Deutsch, Nederlands, Español, Français, Italiano, 日本語, Polski, Português, Русский, and Türkçe.

Bot messages in Discord are configured per server under **Language** and support 19 languages: Arabic, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Czech, Dutch, English, French, German, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Swedish, Turkish, and Vietnamese.

## Profile

Your name and avatar are taken from your Discord account and cannot be edited in TicketCord.

## Billing

**Billing** in the sidebar shows your plan and payment history and opens the Stripe billing portal. See [Manage Subscription](/billing/manage-subscription).

## Your Data

- **Export**: email [hi@ticketcord.com](mailto:hi@ticketcord.com); see [Data Export](/features/gdpr-export).
- **Delete account**: email us; deletion is completed within two business days. See [Delete your account](https://ticketcord.com/help/security/delete-account).
- **Privacy policy**: [ticketcord.com/privacy](https://ticketcord.com/privacy).

## Getting Help

**Support** in the sidebar has the contact email and the Discord invite. Sign out from the account menu.
