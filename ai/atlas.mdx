Atlas AI is the assistant built into the TicketCord dashboard. Ask it about your tickets, staff, and bots in plain language and it answers from your own data.

<Info>
  Atlas AI is available on **Pro** and **Enterprise**. [Upgrade your plan](https://ticketcord.com/pricing) to access it.
</Info>

## What Atlas Can Do

- Summarize a ticket or a whole conversation
- Compare staff response and resolution times
- Report ticket volume, categories, and trends
- Compare your bots' activity
- Draft a reply template in the tone you ask for

Atlas is **read-only**: it never changes tickets or settings, and it only sees data from bots you own.

## Referencing Tickets

Mention a ticket with `@ticket:ID` to give Atlas its full context.

## Sessions

Conversations are kept as sessions:

| Plan | Sessions |
|------|----------|
| Pro | 5 |
| Enterprise | 25 |

A session is kept for 24 hours; **pin** a session to keep it for a year. Start a new session when you change topics.

## Available Tools

Atlas uses these tools to answer:

| Tool | What it returns |
|------|-----------------|
| `getTicketDetails` | Conversation, status, priority, assignments, timeline |
| `getStaffPerformance` | Response times, resolution rates, volume, activity |
| `getTicketAnalytics` | Volume trends, category breakdown, resolution metrics |
| `getBotPerformance` | Server counts, activity, ticket volume per bot |
| `getRecentActivity` | Recent tickets filtered by criteria |
| `generateResponseTemplate` | A reply draft in the tone you ask for |
| `generateTicketSummary` | A structured summary of one ticket |
| `generateConversationSummary` | A conversation summary for handoffs |

## Best Practices

1. **Be specific**: "Which staff member had the slowest response time this week?" beats "How is the team doing?"
2. **Reference tickets** with `@ticket:ID` when asking about one conversation
3. **Start a new session** when switching topics so earlier context does not confuse the answer
