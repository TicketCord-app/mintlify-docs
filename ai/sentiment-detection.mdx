Tone Detection uses AI to read the emotional tone of customer messages and alert a role when a customer is angry, frustrated, or upset.

<Info>
  Tone Detection is a **Pro** feature (Pro and Enterprise). The **sentiment journey** shown on the dashboard ticket view and in Analytics is a separate **Enterprise** feature. [Upgrade your plan](https://ticketcord.com/pricing) to access them.
</Info>

## How It Works

1. A customer sends a message
2. After the analysis delay, the AI classifies the tone
3. If the tone has an action configured, the bot posts a **🎭 Tone Alert** card to your logs channel that mentions the chosen role and shows the detected tone, the confidence, the user, and the AI's analysis

## Detected Tones

| Tone | Typical signs |
|------|---------------|
| **Angry** | Capitals, profanity, threats |
| **Frustrated** | Repeated complaints, impatience |
| **Upset** | Disappointment, sadness |
| **Confused** | Uncertainty, repeated questions |
| **Happy** | Thanks, positive words |
| **Neutral** | Plain factual messages |

## Settings

Open **Dashboard → Bots → your bot → Servers → the server → AI Features → Conversation**.

| Setting | Options |
|---------|---------|
| **Analysis mode** | First message, first five messages, or smart detection (default) |
| **Delay before analysis** | Default 30 seconds |
| **Tone actions** | For each tone: **Ping role** (choose the role) or **Do nothing** |
| **Auto-escalate on negative tone** | Also raise an escalation for angry or frustrated customers |

Alerts need a logs channel configured under **Structure**.

## Sentiment Journey (Enterprise)

With **Sentiment analysis** enabled, TicketCord stores how each customer's mood changed over a ticket and shows it in the dashboard ticket view and the Analytics sentiment section. This history is stored on the ticket and follows your ticket retention policy.

## Privacy Note

Message content is analyzed only to classify tone. Data is not shared with third parties or used for anything other than detection and, on Enterprise, the sentiment journey.
