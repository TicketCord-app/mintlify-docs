The **Learning** tab holds three things: the vote buttons under each answer and what the bot does with the votes, the rules for handing a ticket to staff when the bot cannot help, and autonomous learning from closed tickets. This page explains each one, what staff and customers see in Discord, and how to review or undo what was learned.

<Info>
  Open **Dashboard → Bots → your bot → Servers → the server → AI & Knowledge → Knowledge Base → Learning**. Feedback, escalation, and ticket learning are all on for a newly configured Knowledge Base. Review learned entries on the **Staff Q&A** tab and vote results on the **Analytics** tab.
</Info>

## Feedback: what users see

Every answer the bot posts ends with 👍 and 👎 buttons when **Enable Vote Buttons** is on.

- 👍 records a positive vote and replies `Thank you! Your positive feedback helps improve our knowledge base.` Pressing the same button again removes the vote.
- 👎 opens a menu, **What was the issue with this suggestion?**, with the reasons **Incorrect Information**, **Partially Correct**, **Outdated Information**, **Not Relevant**, and **Other Issue**.
- **Other Issue** opens the **Provide Detailed Feedback** form with **What could be improved?** and **Correct Answer (helps AI learn)**. A filled-in correct answer is saved as a learned entry and the bot confirms with **Correction Learned!** If it cannot be processed right away, the feedback is kept for manual review.
- With **Staff Only Voting** on, non-staff see `Only staff members can vote on suggestions` and their votes are ignored.

Under every answer the bot also prints `Generated by AI. Staff can verify important details.` This **AI transparency notice** is always on and cannot be turned off.

## Feedback settings

| Setting | Values | Default | What it does |
|---------|--------|---------|--------------|
| **Enable Vote Buttons** | On, Off | On | Shows 👍 and 👎 under answers |
| **Staff Only Voting** | On, Off | Off | Counts votes only from users with a staff role |
| **Feedback Learning** | On, Off | On | Boosts or penalises content based on votes |
| **Minimum Votes for Learning** | 1 to 10 | 3 | Votes a piece of content needs before a boost or penalty applies |
| **Good Content Threshold** | 10% to 100% | 70% | Success rate at or above which content is boosted |
| **Boost Percentage** | 5% to 50% | 15% | How much boosted content is favoured in later searches |
| **Poor Content Threshold** | 10% to 100% | 40% | Success rate below which content is penalised |
| **Penalty Percentage** | 5% to 50% | 15% | How much penalised content is held back |

Feedback learning runs in real time: once a piece of content has the minimum votes, its success rate decides whether it moves up or down in future answers. Turn it off if you would rather manage ranking by hand through your sources.

## Escalation settings

| Setting | Values | Default | What it does |
|---------|--------|---------|--------------|
| **Auto-Escalate to Staff** | On, Off | On | When the bot has insufficient knowledge, it escalates the ticket instead of guessing |
| **Ping Staff on Escalation** | On, Off | Off | Mentions your staff roles on the escalation card. If the ticket's button has its own ping role, only that role is mentioned; with no ping configuration, all global staff roles are; with this setting off, nobody is pinged |
| **Ticket Escalation Duration** | Permanent (AI never resumes), 1 day, 3 days, 7 days (default), 14 days, 30 days | 7 days | How long the bot stays out of a ticket after escalating it. It resumes when the time passes and staff have not responded |
| **Human Request Detection** | 10% (sensitive) to 100% (strict) | 80% | How sure the bot must be that the customer is asking for a person before it escalates immediately |

### What happens on escalation

1. The customer asks something the bot cannot answer from your content, or asks for a human (`Can I talk to a real person?`, `human please`, and similar in any phrasing).
2. For a missing answer, the bot posts `I don't have enough knowledge base information to answer that. Use the button below to request staff help.` with a **🙋 Talk to Staff** button. The button is sent once per ticket and works for the ticket creator and staff only.
3. With **Auto-Escalate to Staff** on, the bot posts a **🤖 AI Escalation** card in the ticket showing the customer's question under **User Question** with the note `Escalated due to insufficient knowledge`. When several messages arrived together the title reads **🤖 AI Escalation (Batched)**.
4. When the customer presses **Talk to Staff**, a **Staff Assistance Requested** card is posted (or **Escalated by Staff** if a staff member pressed it) and they see `A staff member has been notified and will be with you shortly!` A second press replies `This ticket has already been escalated to staff.`
5. The bot stays silent in that ticket for the **Ticket Escalation Duration**.

Human request detection applies in tickets and in ModMail conversations.

## Ticket Learning

Autonomous learning turns the answers your staff give into Knowledge Base entries. There is no command to run.

| Setting | Values | Default | What it does |
|---------|--------|---------|--------------|
| **Autonomous Ticket Learning** | On, Off | On | Learns from resolved tickets |
| **Auto-Approve Confidence Threshold** | 50% to 100% | 70% | Pairs at or above this go live at once; lower pairs wait for review. Low means more knowledge with some noise; high is very selective |
| **Notify on Auto-Approve** | On, Off | On | Posts an **Auto-Learned** card with an **Undo** button to the logs channel |
| **Max Auto-Approvals Per Day** | 1 to 200 | 50 | Caps how many pairs go live per day per server |
| **Smart Merge & Dedup** | On, Off | On | When a new pair overlaps an existing entry, confirms the duplicate, enriches the existing answer, or replaces outdated facts instead of adding a near-duplicate |
| **Auto-Apply Merges** | On, Off | On | Applies enrichments and corrections automatically, but only to entries the system learned itself and nobody edited. Everything else becomes a review proposal |
| **Max Auto-Merges Per Day** | 1 to 100 | 20 | Caps automatic updates to existing entries per day |
| **Periodic Consolidation** | On, Off | Off | Periodically merges existing duplicate entries into one answer and archives stale unused ones |
| **Frequency** | Weekly, Every 2 weeks, Monthly | Weekly | How often consolidation runs |

### What happens when a ticket closes

1. The ticket is closed by staff, by inactivity auto-close, or because the user left the server. Tickets with fewer than 3 messages are skipped.
2. The bot extracts question-and-answer pairs where a staff member gave the answer. Personal data is redacted, and channel links are only kept when they point to a channel in your server.
3. Each pair gets a confidence score.
4. **At or above the threshold**: the pair goes live and is searchable immediately. If **Notify on Auto-Approve** is on, an **Auto-Learned from Ticket #123** card is posted to the logs channel with the confidence, category, **Question**, **Answer**, and an **Undo** button. Undo removes the entry from search and marks it reverted.
5. **Below the threshold**, or above it but over the daily cap: the pair is saved as **Pending**. A **Pending Review** card for the ticket (for example Ticket #123) with **✅ Approve**, **✏️ Edit**, and **🚫 Reject** buttons is posted to the logs channel, and the pair appears on the **Staff Q&A** tab.
6. **Overlapping an existing entry**: with **Smart Merge & Dedup** on, a duplicate is dropped, an enrichment adds a detail to the existing answer, and a supersession replaces the outdated fact. Automatic merges post a **KB Entry Updated from Ticket #123** or **KB Entry Superseded from Ticket #123** card with the previous and updated answer and an **Undo** button. Merges that need review appear as **Update proposal** entries in **Staff Q&A** showing the **Proposed updated answer** or **Proposed added detail**; approving applies the change to the existing entry instead of creating a new one.
7. When **Periodic Consolidation** runs, a **Knowledge Base Consolidated** card reports how many duplicates were merged and how many stale entries were archived.

### Reviewing on the Staff Q&A tab

- The counters show **Total Q&As**, **Pending Review**, **Approved** (active in the knowledge base), **Auto-approved**, and **Rejected**.
- Filter by **All**, **Pending**, **Approved**, **Auto-approved**, or **Rejected** and search by text.
- Each entry has **Approve**, **Edit** (opens **Edit & Approve Q&A** for pending entries or **Edit Q&A** for approved ones), **Ignore**, and **Delete**. Entries changed by a merge show **Updated ×2** style badges; edited entries show **Edited**.
- **Create Q&A** adds a hand-written question and answer that is available to the bot immediately.
- **Purge Pending** deletes every pending entry. Approved, auto-approved, and rejected entries are untouched. This cannot be undone.
- Deleting an approved entry also removes it from search. If an entry was changed elsewhere while you were editing, the dashboard reloads the latest version and asks you to try again.

## Viewing feedback data

Open the **Analytics** tab and choose **Source performance**:

- **Total suggestions**, **Total votes** (helpful and unhelpful), **Success rate**, and **Active sources**, with a count of sources that need review because of a low success rate.
- **Source-level performance**: every source with its votes, success rate, and a status of **Needs review**, **Performing well**, or **Stable**, filterable by **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, or **All time**.
- **Top answer sections** and **Answer sections to review**: the individual pieces of content doing best and worst.
- **Feedback reasons**: the share of 👎 votes by reason, so you know whether content is wrong, outdated, or off-topic.

**Pipeline Analytics** on the same tab shows query outcomes and processing metrics.

## Limits and gotchas

<Note>
  Learning notifications and review cards need a logs channel. Without one, learning still happens and pending entries still appear on **Staff Q&A**, but nothing is posted to Discord and there is no **Undo** button; delete the entry on the tab instead.
</Note>

- **Auto-Apply Merges** never touches an entry a human created or edited. Those always become review proposals.
- **Purge Pending** and **Delete** are permanent.
- Corrections submitted through the 👎 form become learned entries too, so review **Staff Q&A** if someone submits a wrong correction.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| No vote buttons under answers | **Enable Vote Buttons** is off | Turn it on; answers posted before the change keep their old layout |
| A customer's vote is ignored | **Staff Only Voting** is on | Turn it off, or accept that only staff votes count |
| **TC018** when voting | Voting is unavailable for that message | See [TC018](/bot/troubleshooting#tc018-feature-unavailable) |
| The bot keeps answering after an escalation | The **Ticket Escalation Duration** expired without a staff reply | Choose a longer duration or **Permanent** |
| Nobody is pinged on escalation | **Ping Staff on Escalation** is off, or the ticket's button has a ping role set to none | Turn it on and check the button's ping configuration |
| The bot escalates when nobody asked for a human | **Human Request Detection** is too sensitive | Raise it toward 100% |
| No **Auto-Learned** or **Pending Review** cards | No logs channel, or **Notify on Auto-Approve** is off | Set a logs channel with `/setup` and turn the setting on |
| Pending entries pile up although staff answers are good | The **Auto-Approve Confidence Threshold** is high, or the daily cap was reached | Lower the threshold toward 70% or raise **Max Auto-Approvals Per Day** |
| The bot learned something wrong | A staff answer in a closed ticket was wrong | Press **Undo** on the card, or **Edit** or **Delete** the entry on **Staff Q&A** |
| An approved entry was rewritten | **Auto-Apply Merges** superseded a system-learned entry | Press **Undo** on the **KB Entry Superseded** card; edit the entry once so future merges become proposals |

## Common questions

**Does the bot learn from every closed ticket?**
Only when **Autonomous Ticket Learning** is on, the ticket has at least 3 messages, and a staff member answered in it. Pairs below the threshold wait for review, and no more than **Max Auto-Approvals Per Day** go live per day.

**Where do I approve what the bot learned?**
On the **Staff Q&A** tab, or with the **Approve**, **Edit**, and **Reject** buttons on the **Pending Review** card in your logs channel.

**How do I undo an auto-learned answer?**
Press **Undo** on the **Auto-Learned** card in the logs channel, or delete the entry on **Staff Q&A**.

**Can customers teach the bot wrong answers through the 👎 form?**
A correction submitted through the form is saved as a learned entry. Turn on **Staff Only Voting** if you want only staff to be able to vote and correct.

**What is the difference between Feedback Learning and Ticket Learning?**
Feedback Learning reorders existing content based on votes. Ticket Learning creates new entries from what staff wrote in closed tickets.

**How long does the bot stay quiet after escalating?**
The **Ticket Escalation Duration**: 1, 3, 7, 14, or 30 days, or **Permanent**. The default is 7 days. It resumes when the time passes and staff have not responded.

**Can I turn off the "Generated by AI" note?**
No. The transparency notice is always shown under AI answers.
