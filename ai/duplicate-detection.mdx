Duplicate Detection uses AI to spot new tickets that repeat or relate to recent ones, so your team can give consistent answers and avoid double work.

<Info>
  Duplicate Detection is a **Pro** feature (Pro and Enterprise). [Upgrade your plan](https://ticketcord.com/pricing) to access it.
</Info>

## How It Works

1. A ticket is created, or its creator sends a message
2. The AI compares it with recent tickets in the same server (up to 1,000)
3. Matches are recorded on the ticket with a confidence score and the AI's reasoning
4. A card listing up to five matches is posted to your logs channel, each with a "Jump to ticket" link

Matches are labelled **repeat** (🔁, the same creator opened the same issue again) or **related** (🔗, a different creator with the same issue).

## Confidence Levels

| Level | Default threshold | Adjustable range |
|-------|-------------------|------------------|
| **Very high** | 90% | 85% to 100% |
| **High** | 75% | 65% to 95% |
| **Medium** | 60% | 45% to 85% |
| **Low** | 45% | 30% to 75% |

## Settings

Open **Dashboard → Bots → your bot → Servers → the server → AI Features → Conversation**.

| Setting | Options |
|---------|---------|
| **Lookback period** | 7, 14, 30 (default), 60, or 90 days |
| **Confidence thresholds** | The four levels above |

## What Staff See

- The **logs channel card** with the matches and links
- The **dashboard ticket view**, which shows related tickets and, for strong matches, a "duplicate of" link

There are no link or dismiss buttons and no feedback loop; staff decide how to handle the tickets.
