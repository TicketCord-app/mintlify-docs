TicketCord runs a Discord ticket bot under your own application, so members see your name and avatar rather than a shared bot. Each bot you create is independent: its own branding, its own configuration per server, and its own ticket history. You configure it from the web dashboard and staff work tickets in Discord with slash commands.

## How a ticket flows

<Steps>
  <Step title="A member opens a ticket">
    They press a button on a panel you posted, pick a dropdown option, fill in a form, or (in ModMail mode) DM the bot. The bot checks the firewall, business hours, and the member's open ticket count first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A private channel is created">
    `ticket-0001` appears in your ticket category, visible only to the creator, staff roles, and the bot, with your welcome message and any staff ping you configured.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Staff handle it">
    Staff claim it, reply (by hand, with `/reply`, or from an `/ai-help` draft), add colleagues, set priority and tags, and keep internal notes. Every action is logged to the logs channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="It closes">
    The creator or staff close it. The channel becomes `closed-0001` and moves to the closed category (or is deleted), the transcript is posted, the creator is DMed and asked for a rating, and auto-close handles tickets that go quiet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the bot does

| Area | Capabilities |
|------|--------------|
| **Ticket panels** | Embeds with buttons or dropdowns, each mapped to its own category and welcome message; optional application forms before the ticket opens |
| **Ticket channels** | Channel or thread tickets, per-user open limits, priority indicators, tags, claims (optionally exclusive), internal notes, close requests, transcripts |
| **ModMail** | Members DM the bot and staff answer from a ticket channel, with evidence tools for moderation |
| **Automation** | Auto-close after inactivity, reminders before closing, auto-close when a member leaves, auto-delete on close, DM on close, feedback collection |
| **AI** | Auto Support answers from your knowledge base, AI reply drafts, ticket summaries, priority detection, translation, auto-routing, tone detection |
| **Enterprise workflows** | Business hours, SLA targets, approval requests, scheduled reports, webhooks |
| **Languages** | The bot's own messages are available in 19 languages, chosen per server under **Platform → Language** |

## Common questions

**Is TicketCord one shared bot or my own?**
Your own. You create the application in Discord's Developer Portal and TicketCord runs it. Members never see the TicketCord name unless you keep the default footer on the Free plan.

**Does the bot need Administrator?**
No. The dashboard invite requests only the permissions the bot uses (channel, role, thread, message, and webhook management). See [Installation](/bot/installation) for the exact list.

**Can I run different settings in different servers with the same bot?**
Yes. Every server has its own configuration, staff roles, categories, panels, and automation.

**What happens when my trial ends?**
The account moves to the Free plan. Configuration and tickets are kept; features above Free stop working until you upgrade, and the bot keeps handling tickets within Free limits.

**Do members need to install anything?**
No. They open tickets by pressing a button or sending the bot a DM, inside Discord.

**Which languages can the bot speak?**
The bot's messages are available in 19 languages: Arabic, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Czech, Dutch, English, French, German, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Swedish, Turkish, and Vietnamese. Ticket translation between members and staff is a separate Pro feature.
