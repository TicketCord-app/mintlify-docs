## Billing Options
### Monthly vs Yearly
Save **17%** when you choose yearly billing:
| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | Yearly saving |
|------|---------|--------|---------------|
| Basic | €3.50 | €34.99 | €7.01 (17%) |
| Pro | €6.99 | €68.99 | €14.89 (17%) |
| Enterprise | €12.99 | €128.99 | €26.89 (17%) |

Prices are in EUR. The [pricing page](https://ticketcord.com/pricing) is always the source of truth.
<Tip>
  Yearly plans are billed upfront for the full year and automatically renew unless cancelled.
</Tip>
## Free Trial
Every new account automatically starts on a **14-day Enterprise trial**. There is nothing to activate and no card is required:
- Enterprise features during the trial, limited to 1 bot, 2 knowledge base sources, and 100 crawled pages per source
- AI usage during the trial is capped at 1M tokens for the whole trial (instead of the plan's monthly allocation)
- When the trial ends the account returns to the Free plan; nothing is charged

<Note>
  Paid checkout bills immediately. Subscribing during the trial replaces the trial with your paid plan.
</Note>

## What Happens When You Upgrade?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Instant Access">
    All new features are available immediately after payment
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prorated Billing">
    If upgrading mid-cycle, you only pay the difference for remaining days
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep Your Data">
    All your existing tickets, embeds, and settings are preserved
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Happens When You Downgrade?

- Features above your new plan limit become read-only
- Extra bots beyond your limit are automatically stopped
- Existing tickets and transcripts remain accessible, subject to your plan's retention policy
- On a downgrade to Free, closed-ticket content retention resets to the Free policy (up to 90 days) for tickets going forward
- AI tokens reset to new plan allocation at next billing cycle
## Frequently Asked Questions
<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change plans anytime?">
    Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing. Downgrades are made through the billing portal from **Manage Subscription**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods do you accept?">
    Cards and Link through Stripe, and cryptocurrency through NOWPayments. Crypto purchases are one-time entitlements for a fixed period; they do not renew automatically.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a refund policy?">
    TicketCord provides immediate access to premium features upon purchase. During checkout, EU customers are asked to waive their 14-day withdrawal right as required by EU law, since digital services begin immediately. Once your paid subscription begins, subscriptions are non-refundable. You can cancel anytime to prevent future charges. See our [Terms of Service](https://ticketcord.com/terms) for full details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What counts as a 'bot'?">
    Each custom Discord bot you create counts as one bot. A bot can be connected to multiple Discord servers (guilds).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do unused AI tokens roll over?">
    Monthly plan tokens reset at the beginning of each billing cycle and do not roll over. Purchased token packages (10M, 20M, 50M) are one-time and never expire.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get a custom plan?">
    For organizations with special requirements, contact us at hi@ticketcord.com to discuss custom enterprise pricing.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
