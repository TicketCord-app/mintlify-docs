## How the Trial Works

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="14 Days" icon="calendar">
    Two weeks of Enterprise features, starting the moment you sign in for the first time
  </Card>
  <Card title="Automatic" icon="user-check">
    Granted to every new account. There is nothing to activate.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No Card" icon="credit-card">
    No payment method is collected and nothing is charged when the trial ends
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What's Included

During the trial you get the Enterprise plan with these limits:

| Limit | During the trial |
|-------|------------------|
| Custom bots | 1 |
| Knowledge base sources | 2 |
| Crawled pages per source | 100 |
| AI tokens | 1M for the whole trial (not per month) |

Everything else follows the Enterprise plan: webhooks, SLA management, business hours, approvals, and the AI features.

<Info>
  If you use up the 1M trial tokens, AI features pause until you subscribe. Everything that does not use AI keeps working.
</Info>

## During Your Trial

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I switch the trial to a different plan?">
    No. The trial is always Enterprise. You can subscribe to any paid plan at any time; paid checkout bills immediately and replaces the trial.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will I be charged automatically?">
    No. No payment method is collected for the trial. When it ends, your account returns to the Free plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my data after the trial?">
    Your tickets, transcripts, and settings are preserved; only access to paid features is restricted. On the Free plan, closed-ticket content retention is up to 90 days for tickets going forward, and bots beyond the Free limit of 1 are stopped. See [Plans & Pricing](/billing/plans).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Subscribing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Go to [ticketcord.com/dashboard](https://ticketcord.com/dashboard) and open **Billing**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Plan and Cycle">
    Pick Basic, Pro, or Enterprise, monthly or yearly (yearly saves 17%)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Checkout">
    Pay by card or Link through Stripe, or with cryptocurrency. Your paid plan starts immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>
