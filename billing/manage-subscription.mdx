Everything about your plan is managed from **Dashboard → Billing**. This page walks through each action exactly as the dashboard exposes it, what you are asked to confirm, and what changes on your account afterwards.

<Info>
  Only the account owner can change the plan. If your plan was granted by a TicketCord administrator, the billing page shows **Admin-managed** and you cannot change it yourself; contact support instead.
</Info>

## The Billing page

Open [ticketcord.com/dashboard](https://ticketcord.com/dashboard), then **Billing** in the sidebar. The page has four parts, top to bottom:

| Section | What it shows |
|---------|---------------|
| **Current Plan** | Your plan pill, then four tiles: **Monthly Cost** (or **Payment Type: Prepaid** for crypto), **Bots Used** out of your limit, **AI Tokens Remaining** (or **AI Tokens: 0, No AI features** on Free and Basic), and a date tile labelled **Next Billing**, **Trial Ends**, **Access Ends**, or **Access Ended** depending on your situation. A **Manage Subscription** button appears when you have a card subscription. |
| **Redeem a Gift Card** | Enter a 16-character partner code to activate a plan. |
| **Plans & Pricing** | The four plan cards with a **Card / Crypto** switch. With Card you also get a **Monthly / Yearly** switch; with Crypto you get an **Access duration** selector (3, 6, or 12 months). |
| **Payment History** | Every payment with description, date, status, amount, and a link to the invoice when there is one. See [Invoices](/billing/invoices). |

Banners appear above the tiles when relevant: "Your Enterprise trial is active" during the signup trial, "Your subscription is scheduled for cancellation" after you cancel, and "Your subscription has been canceled" once the period has ended.

## Upgrade with a card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the plan and cycle">
    In **Plans & Pricing** leave **Card** selected, pick **Monthly** or **Yearly** (yearly is about 17% cheaper), and click **Upgrade to Basic**, **Upgrade to Pro**, or **Upgrade to Enterprise**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Your Purchase">
    A consent dialog opens showing the plan and price ("You will be charged €X for the month/year. Your subscription will automatically renew until cancelled."). Tick both boxes, which start unticked:

    - **I want immediate access to my subscription features**
    - **I agree to the Right to Withdrawal waiver** ("You understand that services begin upon purchase, and you waive your Right to Withdrawal once those services begin.")

    Click **Continue**. By continuing you also agree to the Paid Services Terms. A confirmation email is sent after purchase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    If you have no active subscription, you are sent to Stripe Checkout to pay by card or Link. The checkout page has a field for a promotion code. If you already have an active card subscription on a lower plan, there is no checkout: the plan is changed on your existing subscription right away, with credit for the unused part of the old plan and a charge for the difference, and your billing date stays the same.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirmation">
    You are returned to Billing with "Subscription activated!". The new plan is live immediately; the **Current Plan** card updates and any bots that were **Plan Limited** can be started from **Bots**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  EU law gives consumers a 14-day right of withdrawal on digital services. Because TicketCord activates the moment you pay, the two checkboxes record your express request for immediate access and your acknowledgement that you lose that right once the service begins. Once the paid plan begins, subscriptions are non-refundable.
</Note>

## Downgrade to a lower paid plan

Downgrades are not done from the plan cards directly. When you are on a card subscription, the card for a lower plan shows **Downgrade to Basic** (or Pro). Clicking it opens the Stripe billing portal, the same place **Manage Subscription** leads to. In the portal choose the lower plan.

What changes when the downgrade takes effect is listed under [What happens when you downgrade](/billing/plans#what-happens-when-you-downgrade-or-a-plan-ends): bots above the new limit stop with the status **Plan Limited** (oldest bots stay running), features the new plan does not include are switched off but keep their settings, and nothing is deleted.

## Cancel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the portal">
    On the **Current Plan** card click **Manage Subscription**. This opens the Stripe billing portal for your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel the plan">
    Choose to cancel the subscription and confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep access until the period ends">
    Cancellation always takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for, for trials as well as paid plans. Back on Billing the card shows "Your subscription is scheduled for cancellation. You'll keep access to Pro features until 12 October 2026. After that, your account will be downgraded to the free plan." and the date tile is labelled **Access Ends**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After the period ends the account is on Free. Your account, bots, servers, settings, tickets, and transcripts all stay; only the plan changes. Closed-ticket content then follows the Free retention rules for tickets created from that point on (up to 90 days). See [Plans](/billing/plans#what-happens-when-you-downgrade-or-a-plan-ends).

## Reactivate or resubscribe

- **Before the period ends**: open **Manage Subscription** and undo the cancellation in the portal. TicketCord picks up the reversal and your plan continues with the same billing date; the cancellation banner disappears.
- **After the period ends**: pick a plan under **Plans & Pricing** and go through checkout again, exactly like a first purchase (consent dialog, then Stripe Checkout).

## Switch between monthly and yearly

Open **Manage Subscription** and change the price in the portal.

- Monthly to yearly: the unused part of the current month is credited toward the yearly bill.
- Yearly to monthly: takes effect when the current yearly term ends. There is no partial refund for the remaining months.

## Pay with cryptocurrency

Crypto purchases are prepaid, fixed-length, and never renew. They are handled by NOWPayments.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Crypto and a duration">
    In **Plans & Pricing** switch to **Crypto**, then choose an **Access duration** of **3 months**, **6 months**, or **12 months**. The cards show a one-time EUR price: 3 and 6 months are the monthly price multiplied by the months; 12 months is the yearly price. Click **Upgrade to …**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Your Purchase">
    The same consent dialog appears, worded for a one-time payment: "You will be charged €X once for N months of access. This purchase does not renew automatically." Tick **I want immediate access to my paid plan features** and the withdrawal waiver, then **Continue**. If the price changed while the dialog was open, it tells you and asks you to accept again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a currency">
    You land on the **Crypto payment details** page. Under **Choose a cryptocurrency**, pick a coin and network (for example BTC, ETH, LTC, SOL, XMR, TON, TRX, XRP, BCH, BNB, or USDT/USDC on several networks), then click **Create payment address**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the exact amount">
    The page shows the **Amount**, **Payment address** (with a QR code and copy buttons), the **Network**, and, for coins that need it, a **Memo or tag (required)**. Send only the shown currency on the shown network; a different asset or network may be lost, and a missing memo can prevent the payment from being credited.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for confirmation">
    The status moves from **Waiting for payment** to **Payment confirming** (received, waiting for blockchain confirmations) to **Payment confirmed** (NOWPayments is completing it; your plan is not active yet) to **Payment complete**: "Your prepaid plan is active until [date]. It will not renew automatically." The **Current Plan** card then shows **Payment Type: Prepaid** and **Access Ends** with the date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### The two timers

The payment page shows two countdowns:

- **Amount valid for**: the crypto amount is a quote that is only valid for a short time because exchange rates move. When it runs out, the old amount is hidden "so you do not send the wrong amount" and you click **Refresh amount** to get a new one. Refreshing changes the amount, never the address.
- **Time left to pay**: the payment window is 7 days from when the address was created. When it ends the page says "This 7-day payment window has ended. Do not send funds to this address."

### Statuses that need your attention

| Status on the page | What it means for you |
|--------------------|------------------------|
| **Payment underpaid** | The amount received was too low. The plan is not activated. Contact support; do not send another payment first. |
| **Wrong asset received** | A different coin or network was confirmed. The plan is not activated. Contact support so the payment can be reviewed. |
| **Payment needs support** / **Payment needs review** | NOWPayments marked the payment cancelled, or something needs a manual look. This is not a refund. Contact support before sending more funds. |
| **Payment failed** / **Payment expired** | Nothing was activated. Start a new checkout if you still want the plan. |
| **Payment refunded** | The payment was refunded. Contact support if your plan still appears active. |

Contact support at [ticketcord.com/discord](https://ticketcord.com/discord) or [hi@ticketcord.com](mailto:hi@ticketcord.com) with the order shown in **Payment History**.

### Rules for crypto purchases

- A crypto purchase is a fixed period of access. When it ends the account returns to Free. To continue, buy again or subscribe by card; nothing is charged automatically.
- You cannot start a crypto purchase while you have an active card subscription ("Manage or cancel it before buying prepaid crypto access"), and you cannot start a card subscription while prepaid crypto access is active ("Wait until it expires before starting another paid plan").
- There are no Stripe invoices for crypto; the purchase appears in **Payment History** with the **Crypto** method.
- An unpaid crypto checkout can be hidden with **Remove unpaid checkout** in Payment History. This only hides it in TicketCord: the payment address still exists at NOWPayments, so never send funds to it after removing the checkout.

## Redeem a gift card

Partners sometimes issue TicketCord gift cards. In **Billing → Redeem a Gift Card**, enter the 16-character code (format `XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`) and click **Redeem**. The plan on the card is activated and the Current Plan card shows what you received and when it ends.

## Failed renewal payments

If a renewal charge fails, Stripe retries the card and emails you. While the subscription is past due the account is treated as Free, so paid features and extra bots stop until the payment goes through. Fix the card in **Manage Subscription** (update the payment method, then retry the open invoice). If every retry fails, the subscription ends and the account stays on Free until you subscribe again.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| The plan card says **Current Plan** but I expected an upgrade button | You are already on that plan, or on a higher one. | Choose a different plan; lower plans show **Downgrade to …**. |
| Clicking a plan does nothing and the page says **Admin-managed** | Your plan was granted by an administrator. | Contact support to change it. |
| "You already have an active card subscription" when choosing Crypto | Card and crypto cannot overlap. | Cancel the card subscription and wait for it to end, or stay on card. |
| "Subscription sync failed" after paying | Payment succeeded but the dashboard has not caught up. | Refresh the page. If the plan is still wrong after a few minutes, contact support with your invoice number. |
| Crypto page shows **Payment underpaid** | Network fees or a rate change left the received amount short. | Contact support; do not send a second payment on your own. |
| Bot shows **Plan Limited** after upgrading | The bot was stopped by the old limit. | Open **Bots**, select it, and start it. |

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will I lose my data if I cancel?">
    No. Cancelling only ends the paid plan at the end of the period. Your account, bots, servers, settings, tickets, and transcripts stay in place, and you can subscribe again at any time. On Free, closed-ticket content for tickets created after the downgrade is kept for up to 90 days.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel my trial?">
    The 14-day signup trial has no card and no charge, so there is nothing to cancel; it simply ends. A trial attached to a card subscription (for example from a gift card) is cancelled through **Manage Subscription** and runs to its end date.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I pause my subscription?">
    No. Cancel and resubscribe later instead. Your configuration stays saved in between.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I get a refund if I downgrade or cancel?">
    No. You keep the plan you paid for until the end of the period, then the change applies. At checkout you waive the EU right of withdrawal because access starts immediately, so subscriptions are non-refundable once they begin. For billing problems email [hi@ticketcord.com](mailto:hi@ticketcord.com).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do I enter a coupon?">
    On the Stripe Checkout page, when subscribing by card. Existing subscriptions are managed in the portal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I paid with crypto. How do I renew?">
    Crypto access never renews by itself. When the **Access Ends** date passes the account returns to Free. Buy another 3, 6, or 12 months from **Plans & Pricing → Crypto**, or subscribe by card once the crypto period has ended.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does upgrading mid-month charge me twice?">
    No. Moving up within an existing card subscription credits the unused part of the old plan and charges only the difference. Your renewal date does not change.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
