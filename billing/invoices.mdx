## Payment History

**Dashboard → Billing** lists every payment with its date, description, amount and currency, status (**paid**, **failed**, or **pending**), provider (**Stripe** or **Crypto**), and a link to the Stripe invoice when there is one.

## Invoices, Payment Methods, and Billing Details

Click **Manage billing** to open the Stripe customer portal. There you can:

- Download invoice PDFs
- Update your payment method
- Change the billing email, company name, and VAT number
- Retry a failed payment

Crypto purchases through NOWPayments are one-time entitlements and do not produce Stripe invoices; they appear in the payment history with the **Crypto** provider.

## Failed Payments

Stripe retries failed card payments and emails you. If payment keeps failing, the subscription ends and the account returns to the Free plan.
