---

Scheduled Reports email a summary of your support metrics to the bot owner on a schedule.

<Info>
  Scheduled Reports is an **Enterprise** feature. Each server can have up to 5 reports.
</Info>

## Creating a Report

Go to **Dashboard → Bots**, pick your bot, choose the server, and open the **Scheduled Reports** tab.

| Setting | Options |
|---------|---------|
| **Name** | Up to 50 characters |
| **Frequency** | Daily, weekly (choose the weekday), or monthly (day 1 to 28) |
| **Time** | Hour of the day (0 to 23) in the timezone you pick |
| **Metrics** | Ticket volume, response time, resolution time, staff performance, category breakdown, satisfaction |

Reports are sent to the bot owner's account email as an HTML email. There is no recipient list and no attachment.

## Managing Reports

- Turn a report off with its **Enabled** toggle to pause it.
- Use **Send test** to deliver one immediately (once per minute).
- The delivery history shows each send with its status and any error; it is kept for 90 days.
- Every email includes an unsubscribe link.
