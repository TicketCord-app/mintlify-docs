You can ask for a copy of the personal data TicketCord holds about you. This supports the rights of access and portability under GDPR Articles 15 and 20.

## What's Included

- Your account profile
- Your bots (without tokens or other credentials)
- The list of servers connected to your bots
- On request, per server: tickets and transcripts you own
- On request: login history

Billing records are held by our payment providers and are not part of the export.

## Requesting an Export

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Support">
    Go to **Dashboard → Support**. Under **Your rights** you will find the rights we support and the contact details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Email us">
    Send your request to [hi@ticketcord.com](mailto:hi@ticketcord.com) from the email address on your account. Mention which servers' tickets you want included, if any.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive the file">
    We aim to reply within two business days, and always within the statutory 30 days. If we need something to confirm your identity, we will ask in the reply. The export is delivered as a JSON file.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Deleting Your Account

Account deletion is also requested by email and completed within two business days. See [Delete your account](https://ticketcord.com/help/security/delete-account) for what is removed and what is kept for legal reasons.
