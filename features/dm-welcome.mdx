---
title: "DM on Join"
description: "Send new members a direct message that opens a ModMail ticket when they reply"
---

DM on Join sends a direct message to every member who joins your server. If they reply to it, TicketCord opens a ModMail ticket for them.

<Info>
  DM on Join is available on **Basic** and higher.
</Info>

## Setting Up

Go to **Dashboard → Bots**, pick your bot, choose the server, open **Welcome Messages**, and turn on **DM on join**.

The message is plain text, up to 500 characters. The default is:

```
👋 **Welcome to {server}!**

Need help? Just reply to this message and I'll create a support ticket for you automatically.
```

### Variables

| Variable | Renders as |
|----------|-----------|
| `{server}` | The server name |
| `{username}` | The new member's username |

## How Delivery Works

- Bots that join are skipped.
- DMs are sent from a queue at a steady rate; a member who joins repeatedly receives at most one DM per 24 hours.
- If a member has DMs closed, the message is dropped and nothing else is sent.
- Messages older than an hour that were never delivered are discarded.

<Note>
  There are no delay, condition, or fallback settings, and the DM cannot be an embed.
</Note>
