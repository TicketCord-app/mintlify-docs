---
title: "Auto-Close on User Leave"
description: "Automatically close tickets when the user leaves your server"
---

When a member leaves, is kicked, or is banned, TicketCord can close the tickets they created so staff do not keep working on conversations nobody can answer.

<Info>
  Auto-Close on User Leave is available on **Basic** and higher (and to accounts with grandfathered auto-close access).
</Info>

## Enabling It

Go to **Dashboard → Bots**, pick your bot, choose the server, open the **Auto-close** tab, and turn on **Auto-close on User Leave**. It is off by default.

## What Happens

1. The member leaves, is kicked, or is banned.
2. After a short internal delay (about 1.5 seconds, used to tell a ban apart from a leave), every open ticket **created by that member** is closed.
3. A system notice is posted in each ticket stating why it was closed: left (gray), kicked (orange), or banned (red).
4. Channel deletion and transcripts follow your normal close settings (**Auto-delete on close** and **Auto-transcript on close**).

## What Does Not Happen

- Tickets the member only participated in, or claimed as staff, are not touched.
- There is no configurable delay, and a member rejoining does not reopen the tickets.
- Discord does not tell bots the difference between leaving and being kicked, so a kick is reported as "left" unless a ban follows.
