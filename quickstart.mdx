## Step 1: Sign In

Go to the [TicketCord Dashboard](https://ticketcord.com/dashboard) and sign in with your Discord account.

<Info>
  You'll need **Administrator** permission (or be the owner) on the Discord server where you want to add the bot. Discord also requires **Manage Server** to invite any bot.
</Info>

## Step 2: Create Your Bot

1. Click **Add Bot** in the dashboard
2. Paste your Discord bot token from the [Discord Developer Portal](https://discord.dev)
3. TicketCord validates the token and reads the bot's name and avatar from Discord

<Warning>
  Never share your bot token with anyone. It gives full access to your bot.
</Warning>

## Step 3: Invite to Server

1. Click **Invite Bot** on your bot's page
2. Select the server you want to add it to
3. Authorize the required permissions

## Step 4: Run Setup

In your Discord server, run the `/setup` command. It creates the Discord resources for you, with no prompts:

- The **🎫 Tickets** and **📁 Closed Tickets** categories
- The **📝 Ticket Logs** category with `#ticket-logs` and `#ticket-transcripts`
- The **Ticket Admin** and **Ticket Support** roles

Then finish the configuration (which roles count as staff, panels, automation) on the [dashboard](https://ticketcord.com/dashboard). Use `/config` in Discord for an interactive settings panel.

## You're Done!

Your bot is now ready to handle tickets. Users can open tickets using the panel you create in the dashboard.
